## What Regenerative Agriculture Really Means

Regeneration is more than "organic." It is about leaving the land healthier than you found it — more carbon in the soil, more water held where roots can reach it, and more life in every handful of earth.

## A Simple Starting Framework

1. Keep soil covered with mulches or live crops.
2. Minimize tillage to protect soil structure.
3. Use diverse, biology-rich inputs instead of synthetic shocks.
4. Integrate livestock and crop residue where possible.

## Practical First Steps for Indian Farms

Begin with one acre. Apply Soil Recharge at sowing, follow with Root Guard at the root-development stage, and observe. Most farmers notice earthworm activity and better crumb structure within weeks. Scale what works.
