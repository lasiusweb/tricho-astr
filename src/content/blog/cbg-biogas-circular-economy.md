## From Waste to Energy: The CBG Opportunity

Compressed Biogas (CBG) is produced by anaerobic digestion of agricultural residue, cattle dung, and organic waste. Instead of burning or dumping this material, it becomes a clean fuel and a nutrient-rich digestate — a perfect soil amendment.

## The Circular Loop

- Organic waste goes into the digester.
- Biogas is captured and compressed as CBG.
- Digestate returns to the field as organic fertilizer.
- Crops grow better, producing more residue for the next cycle.
