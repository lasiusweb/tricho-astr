## Meet Trichoderma

Trichoderma is a naturally occurring soil fungus prized by farmers for decades. It colonizes root surfaces, outcompetes harmful pathogens, and produces compounds that help plants absorb nutrients more efficiently.

## What the Science Shows

- Stronger root systems and better seedling vigour.
- Suppression of soil-borne diseases like damping-off and wilt.
- Improved uptake of phosphorus and micronutrients.
