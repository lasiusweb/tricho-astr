## Why Farmers Hesitate

The biggest fear in switching is the transition season — the worry that yields will dip before soil biology recovers. With a planned timeline, that dip is small and short-lived.

## A 12-Month Transition Timeline

- **Months 1-2:** Soil test + baseline. Apply Soil Recharge to begin rebuilding biology.
- **Months 3-6:** Switch basal nutrition to organic sources; use Root Guard for root protection.
- **Months 7-9:** Reduce synthetic inputs gradually as soil health indicators improve.
- **Months 10-12:** Full biological program; monitor yield and input savings.

## Managing Expectations

Expect a transition where the first crop may be similar to conventional, and the second and third crops noticeably better — with lower input bills. Patience pays.
