## Why Soil Health Is the Foundation of Everything

Healthy soil is a living ecosystem. Beneath every productive farm is a vast community of microbes, fungi, earthworms, and organic matter that together determine how well your crops can access water and nutrients. When we treat soil as a living system rather than just a medium to hold plants, yields become more stable and input costs fall.

## Three Pillars of Living Soil

- **Organic matter:** the bank of carbon and nutrients that feeds soil life.
- **Biological activity:** beneficial microbes that unlock nutrients plants cannot reach on their own.
- **Structure:** well-aggregated soil holds water, resists erosion, and lets roots breathe.
